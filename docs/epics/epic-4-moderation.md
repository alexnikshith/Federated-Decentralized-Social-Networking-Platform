# EPIC 4: Safety, Moderation & Trust

## Goal
Provide basic moderation capabilities while maintaining transparency and
instance-level governance.

---

## Features
- Content reporting
- Manual moderation actions
- Admin-controlled content removal

---

## Design Decisions
- Moderation is instance-local
- No global moderation authority
- Transparency prioritized over automation

---

## Phase 1 Scope
- Report content
- Admin deletes reported posts

---

## Future Scope
- AI-assisted moderation
- Trust scoring
- Moderation analytics
